Name & Meaning

O means Ocean, and also a journey from zero

OTools blends the ocean with tools, conveying ecosystem, scale, and the spirit of going from 0 to 1.

  1. O comes from Ocean, symbolizing inclusiveness and abundant plugins; official plugins follow oceanic names to extend the system.
  2. O looks like 0, implying a fresh start and a new platform for the AI era.
  3. Ocean + Tools sounds like OTools; the Chinese name “Sea Rabbit” represents a light, agile tool companion.
